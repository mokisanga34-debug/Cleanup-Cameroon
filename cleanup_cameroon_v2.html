📚 Learn About Plastic & Waste

Knowledge is the first step toward change.

8 million

tonnes of plastic enter the ocean every single year — equivalent to dumping a garbage truck every minute.

🧴
The Problem
Why Plastic Is Destroying Our Planet
Plastic never truly disappears. It breaks into microplastics that enter soil, water, and the food chain — ending up in our bodies. Single-use plastics are used for minutes but persist for centuries.
🌍
Cameroon Context
Plastic Waste in Cameroon
Cameroon produces over 300,000 tonnes of plastic waste annually. Less than 5% is formally recycled. Flooding in Douala and Buea is worsened every year by plastic clogging drainage systems.
♻️
What You Can Do
5 Actions That Actually Work
Refuse single-use bags. Carry a reusable bottle. Buy from bulk markets. Support local waste collectors. Report illegal dumping. Each action multiplied by thousands of people changes cities.
🐠
Ocean Impact
How Waste Reaches the Ocean
Most ocean plastic doesn't fall from boats — it starts on land. Rain washes street plastic into drains, into rivers, and out to sea. Cleaning streets and drains is ocean conservation.
💡
Solutions
The Circular Economy
A circular economy designs waste out of the system — products are reused, repaired, and recycled. Young entrepreneurs in Cameroon are already turning plastic waste into building materials and fuel.
🏥
Health Impact
Waste & Human Health
Open burning of plastic releases dioxins linked to cancer. Waste-clogged drains breed mosquitoes causing malaria. Microplastics have been found in human lungs, blood, and breast milk.
